# Design direction
- Maintain an original graphite and lime dark theme and a forest-green light theme. Share layout, component behavior, and semantic tokens across both.
- Diagnostics separates a status summary, connection selection, and individual HTTP/HTTPS/transfer results. Keep unknown states explicit.
- Preserve the connection wizard, separate fallback editor, and the shared draft workflow. Keep primary actions visible on small screens and accessible by keyboard.
- Use synthetic fixtures and inspect desktop and narrow layouts in both locales. Keep internal visual QA evidence in ignored docs-reports/.
